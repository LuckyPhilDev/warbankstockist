## [1.12.0] - 2026-09-15

### Added
- **Low Stock Warning** A toggle on the Profiles page opens a small window at login listing any tracked item your bags are short of, with the count you have against the count the profile keeps. It closes itself after 10 seconds unless your mouse is over it.

### Fixed
- Shift-clicking an item into the Item ID box on the Profiles page fills in its ID again.
