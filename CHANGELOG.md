## [2.2.0] - 2026-09-26

### Added
- **Always Deposit Master List** A new set every character uses that can't be deleted. Pick up an item at the bank and drop it on the slot that slides out under the Bank Queue window: it joins the list and goes into your Warband Bank there and then. (Thanks for the suggestion Halliday)
- **Active Sets in the Bank Queue** The Bank Queue window lists the sets this character runs.
- **Bank Queue Toggles** Turn off the Always Deposit slot or the Active Sets list in the Bank settings.
- **Withdraw All for Reagent Sets** Switch a reagent set to Withdraw All and a selling character takes every reagent of that category from your Warband Bank, leaving each item's Reserve behind. (Thanks for the suggestion 2B, or not 2B)
- **Housing Dye Set** A new standard set that sends all housing dyes to your Warband Bank.

### Improved
- **Standard Sets List Every Item** A standard set's item list now shows every item it covers, not just what is in your bags, newest expansion first with each item's expansion beside it. The search box works on them too.
- **Exclude Items From Standard Sets** Click the cross beside any item in a standard set's list to leave it out. The row greys out and the item stays where it is; click the plus to put it back. (Thanks for the suggestion 2B, or not 2B)
- **Crafting Quality in Item Lists** Every item list in the settings shows a reagent's quality icon in front of its name, so two ranks of the same reagent can be told apart.
- **Gear Quality** A new option on the Warbound Items set picks the lowest quality of warbound armor and weapons it deposits, so lesser gear stays in your bags to vendor. (Thanks for the suggestion Tuulani)
- **Rarity in Item Lists** Each item's icon has a border in its rarity colour, and items on your own sets come off with a red bin.
